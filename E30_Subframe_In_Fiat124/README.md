Sheetmetal parts for mounting an E30 subframe into a Fiat 124 Sedan unibody chassis. 
This might also work on Lada 2101 or later or Fiat 124 Coupe / Spider. 

# Files
**BottomBox** - Mounts under the car just in front of the back tire. Weld into place. The small hole in sheetmetal is where to drill upwards into passenger compartment. 

**TopBox** - Mounts in the passenger compartment under the rear seat and above the BottomBox. 

**RailNotch** - The stock rear subframe needs to be notched to clear the E30 subframe, this fills in that notch. 

**DriveShaftAdapter** - Adapts the Fiat or Lada four bolt pattern to the E30 Differential pattern. Can be laser cut from two pieces or machined as single piece. 

**Also requires** - Steel tube that fits the E30 subframe bushing that is held captive between the bottom and top boxes.
